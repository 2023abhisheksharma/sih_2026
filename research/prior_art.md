# Prior Art & Operational Systems Review

## 1. Operational Polar Ice Services & Systems

1. **IMO POLARIS (Polar Operational Limit Assessment Risk Indexing System):**
   - Developed under the Polar Code (2017/2018) for ships operating in ice-covered polar waters.
   - Calculates Risk Index Outcomes based on Ice Class vs. Ice Concentration/Type.
   - Standard benchmark for maritime polar compliance.

2. **DMI / Norwegian Ice Service / US National Ice Center (USNIC):**
   - Produce weekly and daily ice charts and iceberg bulletins from Sentinel-1 SAR, RADARSAT, and MODIS imagery.
   - Provide tabular and vector polygon boundaries of pack ice and named icebergs.

3. **Copernicus Marine Service (CMEMS):**
   - Operational ocean analysis, daily sea-ice concentrations, and sea-ice velocity vectors.
   - Standard open data provider across European and international polar research vessels (e.g., *Polarstern*, *Sir David Attenborough*, *RRS Discovery*).

4. **Commercial Marine Weather Routing Software (e.g., SPOS, StormGeo, AWT):**
   - Provide great-circle vs. rhumb-line optimization in open ocean for fuel efficiency and wave avoidance.
   - **Gap:** Mostly lack dedicated high-resolution iceberg dynamic trajectory forecasting and polar sea-ice rheology interaction.
